How should the analysis engine work?

This determines the prediction quality and complexity of the build

A

ML Model (like your screenshot)

Ridge Regression / Random Forest trained on historical data. Factors: USDINR, crude oil, MSCI, market breadth, VIX. Gives numeric prediction with confidence score. Takes more time to build but mirrors what you showed.

B

LLM-Powered (Claude API)

Feed today's data + news headlines to Claude. Get a structured analysis: bullish/bearish signals, key risk factors, next-day outlook in plain English. Fast to build, no training needed.

C

Both — ML numbers + LLM narrative

ML model gives the quantitative prediction (like your screenshot). Claude then interprets today's news and macro context to add a qualitative layer. Best of both worlds.
